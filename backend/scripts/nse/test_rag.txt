Reliance Industries Limited
Q2 FY24 Earnings Conference Call
October 27, 2023

Operator:
Ladies and gentlemen, good evening and welcome to the Reliance Industries Limited Q2 FY24 Earnings Conference Call. As a reminder, all participant lines will be in the listen-only mode. I now hand the conference over to Mr. Mukesh Ambani, Chairman and Managing Director. Thank you and over to you, sir.

Management:
Thank you. Good evening everyone. We have delivered a strong performance in the second quarter. Consolidated EBITDA stands at ₹44,867 crore, up 30.2% Y-o-Y. Our consumer businesses, Retail and Jio, have continued their growth trajectory.

Analyst:
Congratulations on the numbers. Could you provide some color on the 5G rollout and its monetization plan?

Management:
We are on track with our 5G rollout across India. Monetization will be driven by both consumer and enterprise segments. We are seeing good traction in our AirFiber offering.

Analyst:
Thank you. One follow-up on the O2C segment. How do you see the margins evolving?

Management:
The O2C segment has benefited from strong fuel cracks. We expect margins to remain stable subject to global macroeconomic conditions.

Operator:
Thank you. That concludes our conference call.
