
Statement of Human Rights and Principles
(Selected Excerpts relevant to Financial Reporting & Conflicts of Interest)

Principle 2: Financial Integrity
Employees must maintain accurate and complete financial records. All transactions must be recorded in accordance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP). 
- No "off-balance-sheet" entities shall be used to conceal debt or losses without full public disclosure.
- Marking-to-market valuations must be based on observable market data or conservative independent estimates, not internal optimism.

Principle 3: Conflicts of Interest
Employees must avoid investing in or managing partnerships (Special Purpose Entities) that do business with Enron, unless explicitly approved by the Board of Directors and the Chairman.
- CFOs and Treasurers are prohibited from managing LJM-style partnerships that transact with the Company.
