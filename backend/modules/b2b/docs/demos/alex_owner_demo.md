# Demo Script: Alex (Tenant Owner)

## Persona Context
**Alex** is the IT Admin for "Acme Corp". She just received an invitation to the new B2B Platform and needs to set up her organization.

## Scenario 1: Activation & Onboarding
**Goal**: Activate account and access the dashboard.

1.  **Email Client**: Alex clicks "Accept Invitation" in her email.
2.  **Activation Page**:
    -   She sees the "Welcome to Acme Corp" screen.
    -   Enters password: `********`.
    -   Clicks "Activate Account".
3.  **Result**: Redirected to **Dashboard**.
    -   *Narrative*: "Notice how quickly she lands on the personalized dashboard. No manual config required."

## Scenario 2: Upgrading Subscription
**Goal**: Upgrade to "Pro" to add more seats.

1.  **Dashboard**: Alex notices "Seat Limit Reached" alert (hypothetical).
2.  **Navigation**: Clicks **Billing** in the sidebar.
3.  **Billing Settings**:
    -   Reviews features in "Pro" column.
    -   Clicks "Upgrade".
    -   Enters Credit Card details.
4.  **Result**: Badge changes to "Pro".
    -   *Narrative*: "The upgrade is instant. She can now invite her engineering team immediately."

## Scenario 3: Inviting a Member
**Goal**: Invite Sam (Developer).

1.  **Navigation**: Clicks **Team** in the sidebar.
2.  **Team Members**: Clicks "Invite User" (Primary Button).
3.  **Modal**:
    -   Email: `sam@acme.com`
    -   Role: `Member`
    -   Clicks "Send Invite".
4.  **Result**: Sam appears in the list as "Pending".
